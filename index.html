First-week onboarding checklist

REPRESENTATIVE PORTFOLIO SAMPLE

Goal: By the end of week one, the educator can sign in, complete one core workflow, and knows exactly where to get help.

Before launch

  1. Confirm roster, permissions, device, browser, and SSO requirements.
  2. Identify the educator’s immediate classroom goal—not every available feature.
  3. Choose one realistic “first win” that can happen in under 15 minutes.

Guided first use

  1. Model the workflow once in the educator’s own context.
  2. Have the educator repeat it while narrating their choices.
  3. Check understanding with a scenario, not a yes/no question.

Close the loop

  • Send a one-page recap with the three actions they will use first.
  • Make the support path visible and specific.
  • Follow up after real classroom use and address the first friction point.
Support triage playbook

REPRESENTATIVE PORTFOLIO SAMPLE

Principle: The user should never have to guess whether their issue was understood or what happens next.
1 · IMPACTWho is blocked, what can’t they do, and is learning or campus operation stopped?
2 · SCOPEOne person, one class, a group, or the whole campus? New issue or recurring pattern?
3 · CONTEXTCapture device, browser, account role, exact error, timing, and steps already tried.
4 · ACTIONResolve, route, or escalate with a concise record and clear owner.
5 · UPDATEState what is known, what is being done, and when the user will hear back.

Close-out message

Explain the fix in plain language, confirm the user can complete the original task, and document the reusable learning for the next similar case.
30-minute enablement session

REPRESENTATIVE PORTFOLIO SAMPLE

Outcome: Participants leave able to complete one relevant workflow and choose where it fits in their work.
0–3 MINName the user problem and show the end state. Keep feature tours out of the opening.
3–8 MINModel one workflow while explaining the decision points, not every click.
8–18 MINGuided practice with a realistic scenario and visible checkpoints.
18–25 MINIndependent attempt; facilitator watches for patterns and addresses common friction.
25–30 MINCommit to one next use, share the support route, and collect one confidence signal.

Facilitation checks

  • Can participants explain when to use the workflow?
  • Can they complete it without following the facilitator click-for-click?
  • Do they know where to go when the workflow breaks?
